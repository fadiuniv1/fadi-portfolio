About Me

I'm a software engineering student at Tel-Hai Tech College (Oct 2025 – Aug 2027). I care about the unglamorous part of engineering: making systems reliable, making tools smaller, and making work reviewable.

Today I split my time between shipping developer tooling, working on a local-first Android + FastAPI agent stack, and learning in public. I speak English, Arabic and Hebrew.
